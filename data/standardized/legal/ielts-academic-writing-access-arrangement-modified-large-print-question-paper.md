WRITING TASK 1
You should spend about 20 minutes plus your
additional time allowance on this task.
The table below gives information about the
percentage of the population by age group in one
town who rode a bicycle in June 2011.
Summarise the information by selecting and
reporting the main features, and make comparisons
where relevant.
Write at least 150 words.
